360 CyclingMallorca 2027 · The invitation

An invitation to join 360 Cycling’s one-week winter training camp, giving the group valuable early-season training, structured group riding, skills development and team activities ahead of the 2027 racing calendar.

01 · The invitation

The one-week camp

Riders and staff are invited to the one-week camp from Friday 12 to Friday 19 February 2027, seven nights, staying at CM Playa del Moro in Cala Millor. It gives the group a consistent base for training and recovery.

The week is built around purposeful riding, skills development, recovery time and team activities, so riders arrive at the 2027 season with early miles, sharpened handling and a strong team environment behind them.

Parents and guardians are welcome to join us for the week.

Race days

One or two race days are available during the week for selected riders who are at a high enough level, alongside the structured training. Details will be confirmed nearer the camp.

360 CyclingMallorca 2027 · At camp
05 · At camp

Rider checklist

Under 18s

Must be accompanied by an adult

The adult must be available should their child need them.

Arrive with a bike in full and safe working order, carry enough nutrition and hydration for the planned load, carry euros for cafe and lunch stops, and bring appropriate spares and tools for basic roadside repairs. Be ready to ride on time each morning and manage recovery and bedtime professionally.

Before you travel, sort your passport and apply for a free GHIC, which covers state healthcare in Spain. A daily itinerary will follow closer to the time.

Let us know when you have booked

Once your booking is confirmed, please let us know. Any questions, please email josh@360cycling.co.uk.
